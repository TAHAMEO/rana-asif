Practice Areas

Legal Services, Delivered with Care

From the trial court to the High Court — clear advice, meticulous drafting and determined representation.

  • 01

    Civil Litigation

    Suits for declaration, injunction, specific performance, recovery and damages — from trial through appeal and revision.

  • 02

    Criminal Law

    Pre-arrest and post-arrest bail, trial defence, private complaints, appeals and quashment petitions.

  • 03

    Family Law

    Khula and dissolution of marriage, custody and guardianship, maintenance, dowry and recovery of dower.

  • 04

    Property & Revenue

    Inheritance, mutation, partition, possession and land disputes before civil and revenue forums.

  • 05

    Writ Petitions

    Constitutional petitions under Article 199 before the Lahore High Court to protect your fundamental rights.

  • 06

    Banking & Recovery

    Recovery suits and their defence under the Financial Institutions (Recovery of Finances) Ordinance, 2001.

  • 07

    Cheque & Commercial

    Dishonoured cheque cases under Section 489-F PPC, commercial contracts and business disputes.

  • 08

    Legal Documentation

    Drafting and vetting of agreements, wills, gift deeds, powers of attorney and affidavits.

How It Works

Your Case, Step by Step

A clear, transparent process — so you always know where your matter stands.

  1. 1

    Consultation

    Share your matter in confidence. We listen, ask the right questions and explain your options in plain language.

  2. 2

    Case Analysis

    Facts, documents and the applicable law are examined in detail to build a clear legal position.

  3. 3

    Strategy & Drafting

    Pleadings, petitions and applications are drafted precisely, with a strategy built around your goals.

  4. 4

    Representation

    Well-prepared, firm advocacy at every hearing — with an update to you after each development.

  5. 5

    Resolution

    From judgment to execution or appeal, support continues until your matter reaches its conclusion.
